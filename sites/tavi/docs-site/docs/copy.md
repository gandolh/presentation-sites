---
title: The copy rule
description: Anything about Tavi may be invented; anything about watches must be true.
---

The owner's rule for the site's words: invent freely fictive data, but use real facts for real mechanisms.

- **Tavi is free.** His story, his years at the bench, his habits and any quote are invented, and are never a real
  person's data. Tavi's own line in the footer stays a dashed placeholder until he sends it, and the site carries no
  contact details until the owner gives one.
- **Watches are true.** Calibres, brands, part names, tooth counts and ratios, beat rates, history and trivia are real
  and were checked against a source. Where the drawing has to choose, it says so: the tooth counts are the drawing's
  own, picked so the wheels turn once a minute and once an hour, and are not any one maker's.
- **No review step.** The chapters' prose is written by the builders, so the rule is the check.

The copy is English, unlike every other site in the repository, which is Romanian. English prose tools may run over this
site's text.
