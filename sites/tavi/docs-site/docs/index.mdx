**Tavi Ceasornicaru** is a presentation site for a friend who loves clocks and watches and mends wristwatches. It is an
interactive watch movement on warm paper: you wind it, slow it down and take it apart, one chapter at a time. Astro,
React islands and one three.js scene, static, in English.

## The page

One page, seven chapters and one movement. A chapter is prose steps beside a sticky figure; the step at the reading
line poses the figure.

1. **Energy**: wind the mainspring, take the barrel apart.
2. **Wheels**: the going train with each mesh's ratio, then the dial side.
3. **Escapement**: a close-up, one phase of a beat per step.
4. **Balance**: the hairspring breathing and the amplitude.
5. **Together**: the whole movement in 3D, taken apart in seven steps with labels.
6. **The bench**: Tavi's tools at real size.
7. **Repairs**: the footer, with a quiet repair line.

## The ideas worth knowing

- **One part table, two renderers.** The SVG drawing and the 3D scene both read the same typed table, so a ratio, a
  tooth count or a beat is written once. See [the part table](./parts.md).
- **The drawing is always the first paint.** The 3D movement replaces it only where the browser allows, and every
  other case keeps a working page. See [the 3D island and its fallback ladder](./three.md).
- **Figures hold their own state.** A chapter's pose, speed, hold and reserve live on the figure element, so the
  prose script and the control trays share them without a store. See [chapters and islands](./chapters.md).
- **Everything can stop.** Every figure has a Pause, the header has one for the page, reduced motion starts still, and
  sound is off until asked for.
- **True about watches, free about Tavi.** See [the copy rule](./copy.md).
