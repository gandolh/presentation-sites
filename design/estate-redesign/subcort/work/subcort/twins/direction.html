Space, radius, elevation

4 px base; radius 0 and 2; no resting shadow

Spacing 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48. Cards sit 24 px apart on the field, panels 32 px apart on the ground, 12 px on a phone.

--radius-sm 0 for cards, panels and bands. --radius-md 2 for the link button, the menu button and the size picker. --radius-full for the callout circles.

No shadow, resting or raised. A card reads by tone (1.25:1 on the plot), a 1 px edge and its 1.5 px ink top rule. A panel head is a band, 1.14:1 on paper.

The plot: --color-field with a 32 px grid, lines at 1.10:1, half today's step, so the cards and the drawing are the only things that pop.

Not on this site

No input, no error text, no toast and no menu popover: the site has no form, no booking widget and no call button by decision (PRODUCT.md), and the Contact page only shows data. None of them is drawn.
