# subcort: directions for the redesign board

Written 2026-11-13 by the art director. Repo: presentation-sites, site `sites/subcort`, brief 07 (`corpus/briefs/todo/`), which takes its shared steps from brief 05. The other three sites (auto-service, saloon, tractari) have their own directors.

**Recorded calls that bind both directions.**
- **One light look, no theme switch** (`corpus/wiki/decisions-ui.md`, "The phone menus on Drawer", Q18: "saloon and subcort light"; brief 07, Context; DESIGN.md's Light-Sheet Rule). This replaces board-director's light and dark palettes: each direction has **one light palette**, the sketch has no `theme` tweak, `.theme-light` and `.theme-dark` carry the same values, and the sketcher renders that look into every slot name (`light-1280.png`, `dark-1280.png`, `light-390.png`, `dark-390.png`, the `-full` pair). Today's phone contestant is `light-390-home.png`.
- **No calls to action** (PRODUCT.md, "Product Purpose"; DESIGN.md, Don'ts): no phone, WhatsApp, form or sticky bar on home; the phone and mail stay on Contact and in the footer. This departs from research §3's small-business convention (click-to-call in the header) by the site's own decision.
- **The phone menu is the Drawer** (Q40, same decisions page); no bottom bar. **No photography** (brief 07, decision 2). **No dark surface, no resting shadow, no orange fill** (DESIGN.md §2 and §5): structure comes from surface tone, edges and ink rules only.
- **One model.** Both drawings come from `marquee()` (One-Model Rule; brief 07's acceptance greps for `HeroScene` and `ExplodedPlate`). Hallmark 80 and 47c (a drawn subject) are waived on the record: the drawings are the site's only pictures by decision.
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts names are for the sketch; the build self-hosts with fontsource (DESIGN.md §3: no Google CDN).

## 1. Today's strengths (both directions keep them)

1. **The drawing is the argument:** one marquee drawn twice (assembled in the hero, exploded in Plate 01), with numbered callouts whose numbers are the key's numbers. Nothing is decorative.
2. **The title block strip:** the five facts a visitor scans for (span, area, capacity, set up at your place, area served) in one band of cells under the hero, on the first desktop screen.
3. **A register you can follow:** each section opens on a heavy ink rule with its number, name and note in a fixed left column, so the page reads as numbered sheets in an obvious order.
4. **Figures read as measurements:** mono, tabular, with units ("8 – 20 m", "64 – 300 m²").
5. **Calm:** one orange for numbers, leaders and the current place only; no calls, no bars, nothing festive. The restraint is the trust signal (PRODUCT.md).
6. **Familiar chrome:** wordmark left, five places right with the current one underlined; the Drawer on phones.

**What today does badly (both fix).** The header shares the page fill (its edge appears only on scroll), the title block lifts 1.06:1 with a 1.27:1 hairline, and the plates part by rules alone, so the page reads as one undivided sheet (the judges' G1). The hero tent fills 46 % of its box and floats; at 390 it sits in about 300 px of blank. Labels are tracked mono capitals (the eyebrow, plate numbers, datum keys, the caption, "NOTĂ"; Floor 9 and 64), "Subcort · Gorj — Oltenia" and "Gorj · Oltenia" are dot-joined (Floor 10), and the banner is centred against the site's own Uncentred Rule. The key's four 200 px columns wrap "Membrană & acoperiș" to two lines and each note to five or six, at 15 px. At 390 the plate's in-drawing labels scale to about 5 px.
