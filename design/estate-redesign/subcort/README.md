# The redesign: "On your ground"

This folder is the design record of Subcort's redesign. The estate redesign board, where it was drawn and approved, is a private canvas; this is a copy of the app's page on it plus the build behind that page. The owner approved it on 2026-11-13, and [brief 07](../../../corpus/briefs/done/07-subcort-redesign-with-hallmark-on-the-estate-board.md) built it. The code is now the source of truth: where the app and these files differ, the app wins, and later work may have moved a screen on. Read this folder to see what was decided and why before reshaping a screen.

## The owner's decisions

The owner approved the whole board on 2026-11-13, with every question standing at what the board proposed unless they reopened it. The questions this app's page asked:

- **Ask:** 1. Approve B, On your ground, as Subcort's direction, or take the runner-up A, Montaj, mounted? B reopens DESIGN.md sections 2, 3 and 4 (the near-white sheet, Plex Sans for language, the plate-head spine) and stays light. 2. The hero link: keep the neutral "Vezi dimensiunile și dotările" in the hero (the default), or put it back under Plate 01's list? 3. The hero lede is two sentences: keep both, word for word (the default), or end it after "climatizare"?
- **Reopened:** Recorded (DESIGN.md section 2, Light-Sheet Rule; PRODUCT.md principle 5): the sheet is near-white and stays that way. Proposed: the page ground and the hero plot are a pale sage-grey (#f0f4f0, #dfe8de), cards and panels near-white. Still light, flat and one look.
- **Reopened:** Recorded (DESIGN.md section 3): Plex Sans for language, chosen for the comma-below ș and ț. Proposed: Commissioner, whose latin-ext file maps U+0219 and U+021B to the comma-below glyphs; Plex Mono stays for figures.
- **Reopened:** Recorded (DESIGN.md section 4, The plate): the plate-head spine and one asymmetric rule applied everywhere. Proposed: a head band across each panel and cards on a plot. The numbered register (01, 02, 01.1) stays.
- **Reopened:** Recorded (DESIGN.md section 2 and the Don'ts): never fill a surface with the signal orange; today's selected size is an orange chip. Proposed: the selected size is ink-filled with paper text (14.02:1), the orange stays on numbers, leaders and the current place.

Where the files below disagree, the later layer wins: the owner's decisions, then the controller's rulings, then the judges' rulings, then the directions file.

## What is here
