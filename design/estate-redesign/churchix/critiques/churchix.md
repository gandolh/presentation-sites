## The builder's four calls

- **Dropping the kickers is right.** `Rânduiala liturgică`, `Dania ta` and `Pomenire la Sfânta Liturghie` fail Hallmark's eyebrow test: delete each one and nothing is lost, because each lede already says `rânduielile speciale`, `Orice dar` and `vor fi pomenite la Sfânta Liturghie`. Set in sentence case, each would be a third line in the slim band repeating the lede. But they're `kicker` props on `PageShell`, which serves five pages. Despre's `Istoric și hram`, Contact's `Vino la biserică` and the disabled Pomelnice's `Pomenire` go too, and the board shows none of them. List every one, before and after, in the handoff, and remove the prop. On Despre, check `hram`: it's a fact the title and lede don't carry.
- **The `primary-container` flip works, but it's the wrong tool.** Hover and the arch improve, with white text at 13.5:1, and nothing fails AA on the parish seeds. Three things go darker on pages the board didn't draw. The primary `Badge` fill, which is `Fixat` on the Anunțuri list and the article page, goes from #cdb2b1 to #b89e9d, and its text from 9.55 to 7.57. Despre's staff icon discs drop from 5.71 to 4.52. The info `Alert` changes too, though no page uses it today. So the Open sticky's "breaks any user" overstates it. The tokens map marks `--on-primary-container` and `--surface-container-low` as unused by the comps, but four components use them: `Badge`, `Alert`, `PomelnicForm`'s no-endpoint note and `MobileNav`'s ring offset. Fix: keep `primary-container` as today, the lighter M3 tonal role. Give the darker mix its own derived name, `primary-strong`, which is primary 80 % with black, for hover, pressed and the arch fill. That's how `on-primary-variant` and `surface-highlight` were added. No new seed, and keep both tokens.
- **Not shortening the band at 1280 clashes with ruling 1.** The band runs y 73-513, 440 px, against the judged sketch's y 72-483, 411 px, so the cards sit 29 px lower, not higher. The arch grew taller and stayed centred over the giving card, so the 314 px empty column between the name's end at x 535 and the arch at x 849 is still there. At 1280x800 the cost is small. On a laptop it isn't. Fix: issue 5, plus the larger name in regression 1.
- **Keep the extra phone first-screen artboard.** It's the only artboard that shows the bar pinned over the first screen the way a phone shows it, because the full 390 artboard puts the bar at its end. If the board takes one more, make its 360x800 twin. That's where the risk is.

## Priority issues, ranked by cost to the user

1. **P1, Pomelnice takes names it can't send.** This is today's flaw, carried over. With no endpoint set, the comp draws both name fields, the offering amounts and the contact fields. The only block saying sending isn't available is the last one: y 985-1075 at 1280, and after about 1,700 px of form on a phone. An older parishioner types the family's names first. Fix: with no endpoint, open the page on today's note, verbatim, as the first card raised over the band's edge at y 229, info icon in `primary`, with the fields under it. The other option is to treat no endpoint like the "Pomelnice indisponibile" state already on the Direction sheet, with `Contactați parohia`. That changes behaviour, so it needs an Ask sticky.
2. **P1, the 360 fold depends on shrinking the identity.** Fix: at 380 px and under, set the giving card's IBAN on one line at 16/24 600 tabular. A Romanian IBAN is always 29 characters with its spaces, so one size fits every parish, and a 2-line IBAN is harder to read out at a bank. Measured: the buttons end at 701, 35 px clear. With a 70-character name they end at 729, 7 px clear. Spend part of the gain on an arch of at least 96 x 116 and a name of at least 24/30. That clears by 27 px for this parish, and a 70-character name then lands 1 px under. Drop the 74 x 94 arch either way. Past 3 lines of name, the actions fall below the bar at 360 and 390. Ask the controller to restate ruling 2 as holding for the fixture, with the bar's `Donează` as the giving route that is always visible. Never shrink or cut the name to meet it.
3. **P1, the time runs over its column below 1280.** The next-service half is 5 of 11 board columns, and `.when` keeps the day and the 52 px time on one unwrapping line. At 1024 `17:00` crosses the board's divider by 16 px. You can see it touch the rule in `home-1024.png`. At 960 it's 33 px over, and at 1100 it runs 23 px into the column's padding. At 1280 Sunday's own service, `Sfânta Liturghie`, `Duminică`, `09:30` from the fixture's week rows, already runs 2 px past the content edge. The floor checks passed because they test controls, not text. Fix: `.when{flex-wrap:wrap}`, so the day goes above the time when the column is narrow. Measured: no overrun at 960 to 1280 with either service; on Sunday at 1280 the board grows 32 px.
4. **P2, phone headings are bigger than desktop.** It's a cascade bug. `.cx h2{28px}` and `.cx h3{22px}` in the 959 px block share specificity with `.pad h2`, `.iban-card h2` and `.ft h3`, and they come later, so they win. On phones `Transfer bancar` and `Date de contact` render at 28 instead of 24 and wrap to 2 lines. The footer's `Navigare`, `Contact` and `Donații prin transfer` render at 22/28 Inter 600 instead of 16/24, as large as the footer wordmark. Fix: scope the phone sizes to `.sechead h2, .news h3`, or restate `.pad h2, .iban-card h2 {24px/32px}` and `.ft h3 {16px/24px}` inside the block.
5. **P2, Home 1280 has a ragged hero row and a laptop fold.** The giving card stacks `Copiază`, 133 px wide, over a full-width `Sprijinește parohia`, and ends at 731 against the board's 695. Fix: from 960 px use one actions row, as on the phone: the `Copiază` ghost, then `Sprijinește parohia` filled at flex 1, with `margin-top:auto` and `.cols{align-items:stretch}`. Measured: both cards end at 695, the buttons sit at y 614-662 in line with `Programul complet`, and the tray rises 36 px. Then answer ruling 1's "shorten the band" where it matters, with `@media (min-width:960px) and (max-height:760px)` setting the arch to 210 x 252 and its frame to 226 x 268. Measured at 1366x650: the band is 48 px shorter and everything down to the board's bottom edge at y 647 shows. Taller screens keep the 246 x 298 arch.
6. **P2, the second parish holds its contrast but loses its character.** Text passes on the blue seeds, with a minimum of 5.93. The band holds: silver on navy is 4.88, and the arch fill, only 1.21 against the band, is carried by the silver line. The photo slot behaves as it does in burgundy. But the silver seed's OKLCH chroma is 0.015, which is grey, so every gold job goes grey: the bank icon, the Sunday glyph, the logo cross and the current-place underline, at 2.19:1. The Sunday row, #f1f2f4 at 1.12 against white, reads as hover, not the feast. That pulls toward PRODUCT.md's "sterile fintech". Fix: on light surfaces draw glyphs and the underline in `color-mix(in oklab, var(--seed-secondary) 75%, var(--seed-primary))`. Computed, that gives #8493a4 for seed 2, 3.15:1 and pewter blue, and #b18144 for the parish, 3.45:1, a deeper ochre gold. I haven't rendered it, and it changes today's parish icons too, so look before adopting. Both pass 3:1 for icons that carry meaning. Keep pure `secondary` on the band. The Sunday row needs a hue that a grey seed can't give. Mixing in a little primary is the obvious move, but I haven't tested it. The seed 2 render swaps colours only: same name, same placeholder. The long-name probe above is the template test it doesn't run.
7. **P3, 768 to 959 px gets a stretched phone.** At 768 the name is 26 px on one line in a 736 px band. The board is a single 736 px column, with about 400 px empty beside `Vecernia` and the time. The desktop board doesn't fit there; see issue 3. Fix: a tablet pass, with the board and the giving card side by side, each in its phone form, and the name at 32/40. This is untested, so measure it first.

Minor: Title Case survives in `Proiect Special`, `Descarcă Formularul`, `Pomelnic de Vii`, `Pentru Adormiți`, `Termeni și Condiții`, `Politică de Confidențialitate`, `IBAN Donații` and `Contact Preot`, against ruling 6's sentence case. The build stamps `copy: verbatim`, so flag it to the controller and list them in the handoff; I'm not ruling on them. The bar has no `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, though the direction asked for the gesture inset. `Copiază` and `Descarcă Formularul` use the grey `outline` edge, where DESIGN.md's secondary button has a 1 px gold outline and a burgundy label. Grey clears 3:1 and gold doesn't, so it's defensible, but declare it in a Reopened sticky. Footer links are 28 px targets.
