Menu beside the work

The first screen answers what a visitor from Instagram wants to know: what the work looks like, what it costs and how to book. The headline runs across, the photo and the raised price menu sit side by side under it, and on a phone the WhatsApp and call dock is there from the first paint. One light look; there is no dark theme and no theme switch.
