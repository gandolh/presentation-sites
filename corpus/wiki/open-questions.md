# Open questions

## Should the per-site docs adopt the briefs lifecycle?

Resolved on 2026-08-23: they converged on one *shape* (`docs/`), not on this
corpus's *machinery*. `saloon/docs/todo/` and `auto-service/docs/todo/` are still
plain checklists rather than numbered immutable briefs, and churchix has its own
numbered backlog under `docs/todo/` with a `SWARM_PLAN.md`.

Open: whether that machinery is worth adopting per site. Probably not for a
finished marketing site; possibly for churchix, which already numbers its work.

## Do `subcort` and `tractari` stay?

Both are demos, not client work, and both are complete. They cost nothing to keep
but they are the two most likely candidates to move out the way `showcase/` did.
No decision either way.
